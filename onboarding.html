Welcome to Lens Paste

To start using the extension, pin it to your browser toolbar for instant access.

1
Click the Extensions icon in the top-right corner of your browser window.
2
Locate Lens Paste in the list of installed applications.
3
Click the Pin icon next to the extension to lock it to your toolbar.
Quick Search Workflow

Press Win+Shift+S (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+4 (macOS) to capture any region, then click the pinned Lens Paste icon to search it instantly on Google Lens.
